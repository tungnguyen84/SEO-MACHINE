Direct Fitment Verdict: Does the ICECO VL45 Fit in a Subaru Outback?

Yes, the ICECO VL45 fits inside the Subaru Outback cargo compartment with a verdict of PASS_WITH_CONDITIONS. The unit provides 11.6 inches of overhead clearance beneath the rear hatch opening sill (18.5" cabinet vs 30.1" opening). However, two operational conditions must be met: first, the factory retractable cargo cover cassette (16.2" height limit) must be unclipped and removed; second, because the rear window glass slopes inward, the fridge must be pulled forward approximately 6 inches onto the tailgate sill to open its top lid to a full 90-degree angle.

2. Lid Opening Headroom, Slides & Cable Routing

The standard VL45 features a top-swinging lid that reaches an apex of 33.2 inches when fully open. Because the Outback's interior ceiling slopes down to 30.1 inches at the tailgate glass, the lid cannot open to a complete 90-degree vertical lock while placed tightly against the back corners. Owners who prefer full top access without moving the cooler should mount the unit on a slide or consider the newer ICECO VL45 Plus, which features a side-sliding drawer mechanism.

Ventilation Clearance: The SECOP compressor cooling grilles are positioned on the lower left flank. Maintain at least 2.5 inches of unobstructed airspace between the vents and adjacent cargo to ensure optimal heat dissipation in warm weather.
