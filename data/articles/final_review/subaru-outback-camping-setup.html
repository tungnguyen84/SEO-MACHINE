Direct Blueprint: Engineering a Subaru Outback Camping Setup

A properly configured Subaru Outback camping setup requires balancing 75.0 inches of longitudinal cargo length against a 30.1-inch rear hatch opening and a 120-watt maximum rear 12V auxiliary circuit. For Gen 6 models (2020–2025), official SAE cargo volume is 32.6 cubic feet behind the second row and expands to 75.6 cubic feet with rear seats folded. Because the folded seatbacks retain an estimated 3.2-degree forward incline, achieving a flat sleeping surface alongside refrigeration requires a divided two-zone cargo floor plan.

3. Power Budget and Starter Battery Protection

A common error among vehicle campers is plugging a portable compressor cooler directly into the vehicle's rear cigarette lighter socket. In the Gen 6 Outback, this circuit cuts power as soon as the key is turned off. Attempting to bypass this relay with a hardwire mod risks draining the Group 35 lead-acid starter battery below 11.8V, preventing engine startup.
