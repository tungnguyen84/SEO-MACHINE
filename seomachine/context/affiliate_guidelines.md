# Affiliate SEO Content Guidelines (High-Converting & E-E-A-T Compliant)

## 1. FTC Compliance & Transparency
- Every article MUST feature a clear, prominent disclosure at the very beginning, above the fold, before any affiliate links or product mentions.
- Standard text: *"Disclosure: We are reader-supported. If you make a purchase through our links, we may earn an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you."*

## 2. Article Structure for Buyer Intent (Best [Category] Roundups)
1. **Introduction**: Address user pain point immediately. State who tested/researched these products and the selection criteria (testing time, key benchmarks).
2. **Quick Top Picks (The TL;DR Box)**:
   - **Best Overall**: The balanced top performer.
   - **Best Budget / Value**: Most cost-effective pick.
   - **Best Premium**: High-end features for power users.
   - Each with a quick summary and prominent CTA button ("Check Price on Amazon").
3. **Comparison Table**: Responsive HTML table comparing Price tier, Key specs, Weight/Dimensions, Best For, and direct link.
4. **In-Depth Individual Reviews**:
   - Product H2/H3 Title + Award badge.
   - High-res product image.
   - Specifications & Key Features.
   - Why it made our list (First-hand experience & analysis).
   - **Pros & Cons Box** (Green checkmarks / Red crosses).
   - Who should buy this vs who should avoid it.
   - Prominent affiliate CTA button.
5. **Comprehensive Buyer's Guide**:
   - 4-6 critical factors to consider before buying (materials, size, budget, warranty).
6. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**:
   - 3-5 Schema-friendly questions answering common search queries.
7. **Final Verdict**:
   - Wrap-up recommendation.

## 3. Link Attributes & Best Practices
- Every outbound affiliate link MUST have `rel="nofollow sponsored" target="_blank"`.
- Buttons must use high-contrast action colors (amber/orange for Amazon, blue for eBay).
