# Brand Voice & Writing Principles

- **Tone**: Analytical, authentic, trustworthy, objective. Like an expert engineer or seasoned craftsman advising a close friend.
- **Banned Clichés**:
  - Never use: "In today's fast-paced world", "Game-changer", "Revolutionary", "Without further ado", "Look no further", "A breath of fresh air".
- **Realism**:
  - Every product MUST have at least 1-2 realistic drawbacks or limitations in the Cons section. Perfect products do not exist.
  - State specific measurements, numbers, battery life, weight, or test metrics rather than vague words like "super fast" or "very light".
